# Interference Search: compact-state pilot, 26 September 2026

This run began from the gap in the release. The published search merges explicit states and uses a Countdown-specific judge; it does not train a language model to hold and revise many hypotheses. I tested two pieces separately: an exact compact version space for verified cancellation, and a 4B model path that tries to learn content-based inhibition. The second piece failed its mechanism check. No breakthrough claim follows from this pilot.

## Exact compact state

`interference_search/quotient_state.py` is a reduced ordered binary decision diagram. This is classical technology, used here as a reference and a hard baseline. It stores a DAG of shared decision suffixes. A verifier-certified exclusion of a partial assignment removes every full assignment matching it, including assignments never visited. `recover()` finds another survivor without replaying the sequence of exclusions. A differentiable log-partition readout lets a neural model supply per-decision scores while the DAG enforces exact support.

On one synthetic 36-decision case, after 18 certified local refutations, **6,234,837,696 assignments remained in 54 reachable DAG nodes**. Randomized small cases matched brute-force survivor counts, and the differentiable partition and gradients matched explicit enumeration. This is a structured case with local clauses. Arbitrary constraints can make a decision diagram grow exponentially; the result is neither a new compression theorem nor an LLM result.

The scalar reference took about **2.99 ms** for a steady log-partition/backward on the local CPU and **31.09 ms** on the H100 PCIe in that 36-decision case. Each DAG node launches small tensor work, so the GPU loses to CPU overhead here. The symbolic reference should stay on CPU until there is a batched or fused implementation. Receipts: [`quotient_cpu_preflight.json`](../results/quotient_cpu_preflight.json), [`quotient_h100_preflight.json`](../results/quotient_h100_preflight.json).

## Full-checkpoint feasibility

I pinned `Qwen/Qwen3-4B-Base` at revision `906bfd4b4dc7f14ee4320094d8b41684abff8539` and attached the native frontier to it on the user-provided H100 PCIe host. A full-weight AdamW8bit optimizer step completed after a BF16 loss-casting fix. At a shape of a 512-token task, eight 128-token states and four refutations, ten repeated steps peaked at **24.34 GiB** and had a median **0.35 s** step after the first. Both sampled backbone and transition weights changed. These were repeated random inputs, so neither the loss nor the timing predicts useful training throughput or quality. Receipt: [`qwen4b_native_memory_smoke_10step.json`](../results/qwen4b_native_memory_smoke_10step.json).

## Learned inhibition failed

The frozen Countdown curriculum has 2,000 train rows, 200 validation rows, and 200 five-number holdouts. Every row includes a verified dead state reachable from at least two frontier parents. The splits have distinct instances and a five-number size holdout; they share the generator, so they do not establish source-disjoint transfer. Hashes and construction rules are in [`data/native_countdown/report.json`](../data/native_countdown/report.json).

Two matched 4B arms trained for 200 optimizer steps from the same checkpoint, seed, row order, and losses. One applied the refutation penalty; the other computed it but set its output coefficient to zero. The result was identical across arms: **67/220** winning actions on alive validation states and **64/293** on five-number states. Both fell from **80/220** and **82/293** before training. The known-refutation match had effectively zero selectivity, and the inhibitory arm's action-score change became zero. Its raw action-refutation affinity was about **−9,408**, saturating the sigmoid to zero. Receipts: [`native_countdown_pilot_report.json`](../results/native_countdown_pilot_report.json), [training log](../results/native_countdown_pilot_log.txt), [`native_match_diagnostic.json`](../results/native_match_diagnostic.json). Full 4B checkpoints remain on the GPU host under `runs/native_countdown_pilot/`.

I made one targeted repair: normalized successor/refutation vectors, bounded their comparison, computed penalties in FP32, and trained raw match logits with balanced binary cross-entropy. A 25-step check kept the penalty nonzero but did not make it selective. On 30 validation rows, winning actions fell from **29/68** to **16/68**; on the five-number holdout they fell from **27/97** to **22/97**. The mean match gap between refuted and unrelated legal actions stayed near zero. This repair did not pass the gate, so I stopped the fuzzy-inhibition recipe rather than extending it to the rest of the GPU window. Receipts: [`native_countdown_repair_report.json`](../results/native_countdown_repair_report.json), [repair log](../results/native_countdown_repair_log.txt).

The first 200-step run used the raw-dot-product matcher and balanced mean-squared-error match loss. I changed both for the 25-step repair. Its log, checkpoint, metrics and formula are retained, but I did not freeze a byte-for-byte source snapshot before that first run. That is a reproducibility gap for the negative result. The next training run must pin a source commit and write its hash into the run manifest before the first optimizer step.

## What changes next

The direct neural similarity between a proposed successor and a failed state has not learned the content relationship in this setup. A negative coefficient on a weak match is ineffective; its sign is not the breakthrough. The more solid direction is to make verified cancellation and recovery exact in a compact quotient state, while training the model to build useful factors, propose actions, and prioritize computation. For open domains, a failed candidate cannot justify deleting a whole region unless the verifier certifies that region. Otherwise only the tested candidate is excluded.

The next test needs a compositional program-induction family with held-out rules, plus a cached exact solver and a model-without-quotient control. The model must construct constraints from the task and feedback rather than receive oracle factors at evaluation. The claim gate is causal use of the compact state, suppression of unvisited wrong regions without false deletion, counterfactual recovery, and better end-to-end accuracy per total work than the cached baseline. A Countdown-only score will not satisfy it.
